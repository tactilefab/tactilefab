## liblouis 3.38.0 — `vendor/liblouis/`

The braille translation engine, compiled to WebAssembly from the official
source (see `vendor/liblouis/BUILDING.md` for the exact build recipe).
Copyright the liblouis maintainers and contributors.

License: **GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later** —
see `vendor/liblouis/COPYING.LESSER` (and `vendor/liblouis/COPYING`).
The braille tables in `vendor/liblouis/tables/` carry their own license headers
(predominantly LGPL); the Swedish tables are authored by the Swedish Braille
Authority (Punktskriftsnämnden) together with Insyn Scandinavia AB.

The wrapper `ll.js` is our own code (MIT) and talks to the LGPL library through
its public C API. The library is compiled unmodified from the official
liblouis 3.38.0 release. **The complete corresponding source is distributed with
this repository**: `third_party_sources/liblouis-3.38.0.tar.gz`
(SHA-256 `afb26096b18b17f43e6055e6a79ce0058eb9dbdcdcc4597522dcd7f11915ec16`,
identical to the [upstream release](https://github.com/liblouis/liblouis/releases/tag/v3.38.0)).
Replacing `vendor/liblouis/` with your own build is supported and encouraged —
`vendor/liblouis/BUILDING.md` gives the exact recipe (Emscripten 6.0.6). The
JavaScript glue in `liblouis.js` is generated by
[Emscripten](https://emscripten.org/), whose runtime output is MIT-licensed.
